# Silkhand Rovan

## Overview

Silkhand Rovan is the leader and key operative of [the Gilded Knives](../factions/gilded-knives.md).

## Role and Persona

Rovan is an assassin and negotiator: charming and pragmatic.

## Signature Ability

**Exit Clause:** Rovan always has a prepared escape.

## Escalation

- **Early:** Observes.
- **Mid:** Sells information.
- **Late:** Attempts recruitment or ruin.
