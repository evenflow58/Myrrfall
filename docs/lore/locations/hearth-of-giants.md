# The Hearth of Giants

*Colossal stone ruins repurposed into a vertical town.*

Giants once lived here; now smaller folk use ladders, lifts, and carved stairways.

- *Fun:* Vertical combat and exploration

- *Not-happy:* Something still moves in the upper ruins
