# The Verdant Silence

*Villains of Withdrawal and Preservation*

**Philosophy:**

“Civilization is the disease. We are the cure.”

**Theme:** Nature reclaiming space, quiet hostility

**Leader:** *Root-Seer Myr* (former druid of Thorne Bramblecloak)

**Methods:**

- Encroaching wild zones

- Sabotage of infrastructure

- Forced evacuations

**Where They Operate:**

- Verdant Rise

- The Hearth of Giants outskirts

**Encounter Style:**

- Terrain manipulation

- Ambush predators and plant constructs

**Clock Impact:**

- Stabilizes nature but destabilizes society

- Complicates alliances with Thorne

## Kess Rootbound — Voice of the Growing Quiet

**Role:** Ambusher, terrain manipulator

**Persona:** Soft-spoken, absolute

**Tactics:**

- Hit-and-run attacks

- Battlefield reshaping mid-fight

**Evolution:**

- *Early:* Guerrilla druid

- *Mid:* Fuses with awakened terrain

- *Late:* Becomes a mobile biome

**Signature Ability:** *Encroaching Green* — Terrain expands each round

**Clock Interaction:**

- Stabilizes wild zones

- Destabilizes settlements
