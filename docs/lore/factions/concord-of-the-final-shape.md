# The Concord of the Final Shape

*Villains of Control and Order*

**Philosophy:**

“A stable world requires a single guiding will.”

**Theme:** Law, inevitability, strategic cruelty

**Leader:** *The Harmon Archon* (public figurehead; secretly guided by Aurelion Vex)

**Methods:**

- Political manipulation

- Magical contracts and binding oaths

- Restructuring cities “for their own good”

**Where They Operate:**

- Luminara (Sunspire Ward, quietly)

- Clockfall Vale (time-regulation experiments)

**Encounter Style:**

- Elite, disciplined enemies

- Battlefield control, suppression magic

**Clock Impact:**

- Advances the **Resurgence Clock** steadily and quietly

- Accelerates the **Revelation Clock** if their rhetoric is examined closely
