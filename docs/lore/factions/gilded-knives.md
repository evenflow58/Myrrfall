# The Gilded Knives

## Overview

The Gilded Knives mirror [Maelis Quill](../heroes/maelis-quill.md). Their philosophy is: **“The world ends; profit from it.”**

Their encounters emphasize traps, poisons, mobility, and social situations that turn violent. The faction can slow campaign clocks temporarily while worsening long-term threats.

## Leadership

[Silkhand Rovan](../villains/silkhand-rovan.md) is the faction’s named leader and key operative.
