# The Choir of the Drowned Laugh

*Villains of Denial and Madness*

**Philosophy:**

“If the world is a joke, why not laugh?”

**Theme:** Illusion, memory, despair masked as joy

**Leader:** *The Bell-Toll Matron* (bound spirit from the Laughing Deep)

**Methods:**

- Reality-warping performances

- Memory alteration

- Weaponized humor and fear

**Where They Operate:**

- The Laughing Deep

- Traveling performances across regions

**Encounter Style:**

- Psychic damage

- Unreliable perceptions

**Clock Impact:**

- Advances **Legacy** and **Revelation** clocks together

- Exposes the cost of ignored trauma

## Bell-Touched Lysa — The Smiling Echo

**Role:** Illusionist, psychic disruptor

**Persona:** Cheerful, unsettlingly sincere

**Tactics:**

- Splits the party’s perceptions

- Weaponizes humor and nostalgia

**Evolution:**

- *Early:* Street performer spreading influence

- *Mid:* Cult figure with warped followers

- *Late:* Living conduit to the Laughing Deep

**Signature Ability:** *Shared Punchline* — Pain and confusion ripple across targets

**Clock Interaction:**

- Advances **Legacy** and **Revelation** together
