# The Ashen Remnant

*Villains of Unleashed Power*

**Philosophy:**

“If destruction is inevitable, let it be magnificent.”

**Theme:** Fire, rebirth, fanaticism

**Leader:** *Cinder-Speaker Kael* (believes Seraphine is destiny incarnate)

**Methods:**

- Ritual burnings

- Summoning elemental catastrophes

- Worship of magical devastation

**Where They Operate:**

- [The Prism Expanse](../world/prism-expanse.md)

- [Stormreach Carnival](../locations/stormreach-carnival.md) (behind the scenes)

**Encounter Style:**

- High-damage enemies

- Volatile terrain, chain reactions

**Clock Impact:**

- Advances the **Resurgence Clock** rapidly

- Can force Seraphine to intervene
