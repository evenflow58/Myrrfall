# Faction Map

## Myrrfall Campaign Matrix

| Fallen Dawn Hero | Public Legend | True Nature | Villain Faction | Villain Philosophy | Clock Impact | Party Encounter Styles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aurelion Vex — The Radiant Strategist | Brilliant commander who unified the Dawn | Heroism without control is chaos; orchestrates a controlled apocalypse | Concord of the Final Shape | A stable world requires a single guiding will | Resurgence ↑ steadily; Revelation ↑ if rhetoric examined | Elite, disciplined enemies; battlefield control; political manipulation |
| Seraphine Emberfall — The Living Inferno | Sorcerer who burned entire armies | Fears own power; self-exiled | The Ashen Remnant | If destruction is inevitable, let it be magnificent | Resurgence ↑ rapidly; forces Seraphine choices | High-damage enemies; volatile terrain; chain reactions |
| Thorne Bramblecloak — The Verdant Warden | Protector of cities and wilds | Prioritizes nature over civilization; merged with Verdant Rise | The Verdant Silence | Civilization is the disease; nature is the cure | Stabilizes nature; destabilizes society | Terrain manipulation; ambush predators; plant constructs |
| Maelis Quill — The Laughing Shadow | Always escaped; smiling rogue | Survival above all; created contingencies that empower villains | The Gilded Knives | The world ends; profit from it | Slows clocks temporarily; worsens long-term threats | Traps, poisons, mobility; social encounters turning violent |
| Elyndra Vale — The Last Light | Heart of the Dawn; kept them united | Knows Aurelion’s plan; moral hesitation allows it to continue | The Shattered Tome | Truth is a weapon; use it recklessly | Revelation ↑ rapidly; forces morally fraught choices | Social puzzles; deception-based challenges; magical text/illusion traps |
