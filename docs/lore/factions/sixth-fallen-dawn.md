# The Sixth Fallen Dawn

## Campaign Addendum

> **Status:** Long-term mystery — do not reveal or resolve soon  
> **Origin:** The Thirteenth Hour / Refractions Reach one-shot  
> **Known fact:** The historical record says there were five Fallen Dawn heroes. There was actually a sixth.

---

## Core Mystery

The Fallen Dawn were not always five.

A sixth member existed, but their identity, name, role, and ultimate fate have been deliberately erased from history.

The existence of this sixth hero was uncovered by Tordek beneath the clock tower in Refractions Reach after the destruction of the Thirteenth Hour.

A hidden chamber contained representations of the five known Fallen Dawn heroes:

- Aurelion Vex
- Seraphine Emberfall
- Thorne Bramblecloak
- Maelis Quill
- Elyndra Vale

However, the chamber contained **six slots**.

The sixth slot was empty.

Above it was carved:

> **THE ONE WHO WALKED AWAY**

This should remain unexplained for a long time.

---

## What Tordek Knows

Tordek has seen a magical recording of Maelis Quill connected to the chamber.

The recording implied that the Thirteenth Hour was created because the Fallen Dawn needed to hide something—or someone.

Tordek also saw a brief vision of the Fallen Dawn standing together with a **sixth figure**.

The sixth figure walked away from the others.

When the figure turned toward Tordek, their face could not be seen.

The figure said:

> **"Not yet."**

Tordek does not know who the sixth figure was, why they left, or why the other five erased their existence.

---

## What Is Actually True

**Keep this section DM-only.**

The sixth member should eventually become an important piece of the Fallen Dawn story, but their identity and purpose should not be revealed anytime soon.

The exact truth is intentionally left open for future development.

Possible directions include:

- The sixth member discovered something about the Fallen Dawn that the other five wanted hidden.
- The sixth member opposed a decision made by the other five.
- The sixth member survived the Fallen Dawn but deliberately disappeared.
- The sixth member became connected to one of the major factions or threats in the modern era.
- The sixth member's actions were responsible for part of the damage caused by the Fallen Dawn.
- The sixth member may have been the person who originally understood what the Thirteenth Hour actually was.

Do not commit to one of these until it naturally fits the campaign.

---

## Long-Term Foreshadowing

Use extremely subtle references rather than direct reveals.

Possible clues:

### Ancient Records
An old document occasionally refers to a group of **six**, but later copies have been altered to say five.

### Art
A mural depicting the Fallen Dawn contains an obvious gap where another person should stand.

### Statues
A ruined monument has five recognizable statues and one empty pedestal.

### Symbols
An unfamiliar sixth symbol occasionally appears alongside the known Fallen Dawn symbols.

### NPC Knowledge
An ancient or unusually knowledgeable NPC may hesitate when saying "the five heroes."

### Magic
Certain Fallen Dawn artifacts may react as though they recognize a sixth person.

### Tordek's Sword
The Remembered Blade may occasionally show Tordek brief images involving the sixth figure.

These should be rare enough that the players initially assume they are unrelated details.

---

## Important Campaign Rule

**Do not pay this mystery off soon.**

The sixth Fallen Dawn member should function as a background mystery that gradually becomes more significant.

Avoid:

- Revealing their name immediately
- Introducing them as a current NPC
- Explaining why they disappeared
- Making them the villain too early
- Having an NPC simply tell the party that there were six
- Turning every Fallen Dawn clue into evidence about the sixth member

The goal is for the players to eventually realize:

> "Wait... we've been seeing references to this person for a very long time."

---

## First Confirmed Discovery

**Session:** The Thirteenth Hour  
**Location:** Refractions Reach  
**Character who discovered it:** Tordek

This is the first confirmed evidence that the historical account of the Fallen Dawn is incomplete.

Tordek should be able to remember the discovery, but the rest of the party does not necessarily need to learn about it immediately.

---

## Future Hook

At some point much later in the campaign, a piece of evidence should finally make the sixth member undeniable.

A good reveal would be a document, artifact, or location that contains the sixth member's **name and symbol together** for the first time.

That moment should answer one question while creating several more.

**Do not resolve the entire mystery at once.**
