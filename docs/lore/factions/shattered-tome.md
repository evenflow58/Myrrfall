# The Shattered Tome

Aligned against: **Elyndra Vale**

**Core Identity**

- Villains of secrets, misinformation, and revelation

- Philosophy: “Truth is a weapon; use it recklessly.”

- Theme: Knowledge twisted into power, lies masquerading as wisdom

- Leader: Archivist of Broken Codices (claims to preserve history, actually distorts it)

**Methods**

- Forging false histories and “lost” texts

- Inciting panic or manipulation through misinterpreted knowledge

- Sabotaging Elyndra’s archival work to hide or twist information

- Manipulating scholars, historians, and magical wards

**Primary Areas of Operation**

- Hidden libraries and ruined archives across Myrrfall

- Luminara (secret compartments beneath the Head Archivist’s tower)

**Encounter Style**

- Social puzzles and deception-based challenges

- Traps that rely on misdirection or false information

- Magical constructs or wards tied to corrupted texts

**Clock Effects**

- Advances the Revelation Clock by forcing premature or misleading discoveries

- Can indirectly push the Resurgence Clock if the party acts on false knowledge
