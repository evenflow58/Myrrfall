# The Prism Jacks

## Salvage Runners, Territorial Toughs, Opportunistic Survivors

The Prism Jacks are not a criminal empire or secret cult. They’re locals who survive by scavenging unstable zones before officials lock them down. They know the streets, the salvage routes, and which pylons are “safe enough” to approach.

To them, outsiders are dangerous because:

- outsiders stir things up

- investigations shut down profitable areas

- stabilization crews attract attention

- “heroes” tend to break things

They are rough, defensive, and pragmatic—not evil.

### First Encounter Scene

#### Best Locations

- Alley between Shattermarket and Coil Yard

- Near a salvage route outside town

- Blocking access to a “restricted” crystal collapse

### Read-Aloud

“A loose ring of figures steps into the street ahead of you, boots crunching against cracked prismglass. Their coats are patched with scavenged insulation cloth and crystal plating, and each carries tools that look halfway between mining gear and weapons.

One of them twirls a hooked salvage rod against his shoulder.

‘You’re asking a lot of questions for people who just got here.’”

### Group Composition (Balanced for 3 Level 1 PCs)

#### Rell Vex — Jack Lead

Human scavenger, late 20s, sharp-eyed, confident.

Not a killer. Very willing to threaten.

He wants:

- the party to stop poking around

- access to unstable zones preserved

- salvage routes left alone

#### Personality

- Casual intimidation

- Smirks constantly

- Talks like he already knows how this ends

**Sample Lines**

“You think the Reach survives because the smart people run it?”

“Everybody here eats because something out there breaks.”

“Go chase storms somewhere else.”

#### Encounter Structure

This should begin as:

Social Pressure → Escalation → Optional Fight

Players should have multiple ways through:

- persuasion

- intimidation

- bribery

- gambling/challenge

- combat

#### SOCIAL ENCOUNTER

Goals

The Jacks want the party to:

- stop investigating

- avoid certain salvage zones

- leave “official business” alone

Skill Checks

| Action | DC | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persuasion | 12 | Rell eases off and talks |
| Intimidation | 13 | Jacks back down slightly but become hostile later |
| Insight | 11 | Rell is more protective than malicious |
| Investigation | 12 | Their gear contains official salvage markings |
| Bribery (10–20g or salvage) | Automatic | They become cooperative |

#### If They've Already Been Intimidated (Current Campaign)

The party has already met the Jacks and intimidated them into backing down without a fight. Per the table above, that left the Jacks hostile for later — this is a rematch, not a first meeting. Rell was embarrassed in front of his crew and has prepared for this party specifically:

- **Repeat Intimidation: DC 16**, and Rell has advantage on checks and saves against being intimidated or frightened by this party. The same trick does not work twice.
- **Persuasion: DC 13** — he is nursing a grudge, but he is still protective rather than malicious.
- **Bribery:** costs more now — 25–40g or genuinely valuable salvage. Pride is expensive.
- Storm Dice is still an option if the party talks first: Rell would rather win his pride back at dice than bleed for it, but he plays sharper than he did the first time.

Successful Social Outcome

The Jacks:

- warn party about unstable zones

- mention “patterns changing”

- share hidden salvage routes

Reward:

- 1 Prismglass Fragment

- OR 1 Damaged Pylon Component

#### OPTIONAL MINI-GAME

“Storm Dice”

If players avoid fighting: Rell proposes a gambling challenge instead.

Rules

Roll 3 rounds:

- Each player rolls:

  - Sleight of Hand

  - Deception

  - Insight

Best 2 of 3 wins.

Stakes

If party wins:

- Jacks leave peacefully

- gain salvage info

If Jacks win:

- party agrees to stay out of a zone temporarily

#### Level 2 Rematch — Scaled Combat (3 Level 2 PCs)

Use this version when the Jacks come back hostile after having been intimidated by this party (see the social note above). Fought straight it is a hard fight; the morale rules below are what keep it medium-hard in practice.

**Composition:** Rell + 3 Salvagers. If the party is already wounded, use Rell + 2 Salvagers — that is the difficulty dial.

**Setup:** Rell and 1 Salvager in melee range; 2 Salvagers on shard cover with slings out. The Jacks pick shard-strewn ground on purpose: prismglass ground is difficult terrain for the party, but not for the Jacks (Salvage Boots, below).

⚖️ Scaled Combat Guidance — Morale

- A Salvager scatters (flees or surrenders) at **5 HP or less**.

- At **12 HP or less**, Rell calls the scatter and burns his last Flash Shard to cover the retreat.

- The Jacks are still not killers: a downed PC is knocked out and relieved of salvage, not finished off. Losing costs the party loot and pride, not a character.
