# Echo Crucible: Final Encounter

**Scene Setup:**  After passing the Memory Gate, the party enters a **wide crystalline cavern**, the walls pulsing with refracted light and residual spell echoes. At the center lies a **floating crystalline Core**, fractured and vibrating with unstable magical energy. Sparks of residual magic leap from it, forming ghostly echoes of past spellcasters’ attempts to stabilize it.

Read-Aloud: “The cavern stretches wide, its walls alive with flickering light. At its center floats a jagged crystal sphere, spinning and cracking in unstable harmony. Magical echoes pulse outward, and every step closer sends tiny shards leaping from the floor. This… is the heart of the Echo Crucible.”

## Encounter Design

**Core Mechanics:**

1. **The Core:**

  - AC: 15

  - HP: 60 (adjust for party level)

  - Vulnerable to stabilization skills: Arcana, Tinkering, Nature, or even spell-based dampening.

  - Every turn the Core pulses (roll 1d4) — small wild surge effects happen: damage, area disruption, or temporary chaos.

1. **Add Waves (Echo Constructs):**

  - Every 2 rounds, 1d3 minor shard or elemental creatures spawn near the Core.

  - AC 12, HP 10, small melee or ranged attacks (1d6 damage).

  - They are semi-hostile echoes, tied to past spell failures, but **non-lethal if the party wants to avoid them**.

  - Optional: allow Arcana/Performance/Nature checks to **dampen/add energy to slow or redirect them**.

### Turn Structure

**Each Round:**

1. **Core Pulse:** Roll 1d4

  - 1: Small shockwave — everyone within 10 ft must make Dex DC 12 or fall prone.

  - 2: Residual magic surge — random 5-ft square becomes hazardous (1d4 damage or difficult terrain).

  - 3: Pulse of echoes — creates phantom visual distractions; Perception DC 12 to avoid disadvantage on attack or skill check.

  - 4: Unstable burst — Arcana DC 12 or Nature DC 12 to stabilize the Core before the next round, or the Core loses 1 stability point (adds to difficulty).

1. **Add Spawns:** 1d3 minor enemies enter the field near the Core.

1. **Player Actions:**

  - **Core Stabilizers:** Use Arcana, Tinkering, Nature, Spellcasting, or creative solutions to stabilize energy.

    - Each success: 1 stability token.

    - **Goal:** 6 stability tokens to fully stabilize the Core.

  - **Fighters / Defenders:** Deal with adds to protect stabilizers or slow enemy approach.

## 🏆 Victory & Salvage

**If Core Stabilized:**

- Magical echoes calm. Light refracts harmoniously.

- **Salvage drops:**

  - **Calibrated Flux Gauge (trade / schematic)**

  - **Stormline Compass (1 salvage)**

  - Minor Prismglass Fragments for Shattermarket trade

**If Core Partially Stabilized:**

- Core hums but still pulses irregularly; adds stop spawning, but residual magic causes ongoing minor hazards.

- **Salvage drops:**

  - Bottled Surge (1 vial)

  - Unstable Glasswood fragment

**If Core Fails:**

- Core erupts violently; small crystal shards shoot out (1d6 damage), room partially damaged.

- Iri’s wild magic flares, creating a temporary **magical hazard** or minor creature (like a shard elemental).

- Party can retreat or try a risky “catch-up stabilization” with a skill challenge.
