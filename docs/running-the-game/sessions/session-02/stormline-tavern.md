# The Stormline Tavern

The Stormline Tavern leans slightly, as though it has weathered more than wind.

Warm light spills from its windows, and the low murmur of voices rolls out with the smell of spiced ale and hot iron. Inside, the walls are lined with cracked crystal trophies and salvaged pylon fragments, each mounted like a hunting prize.

Patrons speak in lowered tones — prospectors, researchers, mercenaries — all glancing now and then toward the distant storm visible through the back windows.

Here, the storm feels far enough away to drink to surviving it.

**NPC Energy:**

- Prospectors bragging about “nearly stable” crystal finds.

- A researcher nursing a drink after losing equipment.

- A courier refusing to travel toward Shardfall Fields.

**Rumors:**

- “Something big moves inside the storm.”

- “Lightning struck the same place three times in a perfect circle.”

- “The pylon out there hums at night, even when readings say it shouldn’t.”

## Captain Rhoric Vale — Storm Prospector

**Race:** Human **Energy:** Weathered, confident, slightly reckless **Role:** Field guide who has survived the Shardfall Fields

Rhoric leads salvage teams into unstable zones.

He:

- Lost half his crew last season in a pylon harmonic overload.

- Still goes back.

He claims he saw:

“Lightning folds around something. Not strike it. Around it.”

He describes it like wings.

He drinks heavily after telling that story.

He can:

- Guide them to Shardfall.

- Warn them where the storm shifts unpredictably.

- Offer a side contract to retrieve a lost team’s stabilizer core.

He believes something in the storm is learning.
