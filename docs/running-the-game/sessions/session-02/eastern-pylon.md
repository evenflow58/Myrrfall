# The Eastern Pylon

The ground ahead fractures into a jagged clearing where the eastern pylon leans at a dangerous angle, its crystal rings misaligned and pulsing with uneven light. Thin arcs of energy snap between nearby shards, and the air hums with a rising, discordant tone that vibrates in your chest. With each pulse, the surrounding glasswood bends slightly inward—as if the land itself is being pulled out of harmony.

## Location & Setup

- The pylon is **half-buried in fractured crystal terrain**, humming with unstable resonance.

- Magic pulses in **rhythmic waves**, visible as shifting patterns on the ground and in the air.

- Several **small wild magic phenomena** (mini hazards) appear, instead of a single creature:

  - **Crystalline wisps** (harmless visually, but if touched → minor surge)

  - **Glasswood spikes** that curve unpredictably

  - **Arcane feedback loops** in the crystal lattice

- The storm above the pylon is **centered and fixed**, not moving, but lightning arcs down in small, scattered bursts.

### Core Objective

Stabilize the pylon by **recalibrating the harmonic flow**, which requires:

1. **Identifying the problem nodes** (the altered crystal rings Velrix inserted).

1. **Synchronizing resonance frequencies** across three points around the pylon.

1. **Safely re-routing excess magical energy** into safe conduits.

### Mechanics — Multi-Step Skill Challenge

- Use a **3-step skill challenge**, but vary the skills to make it feel different from the Shardfall cloaker fight:

#### Step 1 — Scan & Analyze the Nodes

- Players inspect the pylon, trying to locate the destabilized crystals.

- **Primary Skills:** Arcana DC 13, Investigation DC 13, Insight DC 12

- **Hazard:** Unstable nodes pulse randomly; failure → 1d4 lightning damage to one character, or the node misfires and requires another check.

- **Optional:** Player can spend a success token from travel encounters to automatically locate one node.

#### Step 2 — Synchronize Resonance

- The three unstable nodes must resonate together.

- **Primary Skills:** Arcana DC 13 (tune energy), Performance DC 12 (guide harmonic vibrations), Tinker’s Tools DC 12 (physically adjust crystal mounts)

- **Hazard:** Nodes spike if tuned incorrectly; failure → small local surge (e.g., knockback 5 ft, minor equipment damage, or area difficult terrain).

- **Flavor:** Color pulses ripple along the crystal, creating a visual pattern — if they fail repeatedly, the pylon “screams” in harmonic feedback.

#### Step 3 — Reroute Excess Energy

- Excess energy must be channeled safely into **three magical conduits**, otherwise the pylon will explode or release uncontrolled surges.

- **Primary Skills:** Arcana DC 13, Athletics DC 12 (physically stabilize conduits), Survival DC 12 (route energy along natural patterns), Sleight of Hand DC 12 (fine adjustments)

- **Hazard:** Each failure → a small surge pulse (1d4 lightning/force) or causes one conduit to destabilize, increasing difficulty for the next step.

### Optional Mini-Challenges

- **Mini Hazards / “Creatures”:**

  - **Shardlings:** tiny crystal creatures attracted to the pylon’s erratic energy; can be mollified (Persuasion DC 12) or avoided.

  - **Resonance Wisps:** harmless floating motes; if touched, create a small magical backlash (Dexterity DC 12 to dodge).

- These hazards are **environmental distractions**, not a fight, giving players a sense of risk without a major monster.

### Outcomes

- **Full Success:** Pylon stabilizes, local wild magic pulses quiet, and the party gains a **refined salvage reward**:

  - 1 intact shard (Resonance Chime)

  - 1 prismglass fragment (Crystal Patch Kit)

  - Minor temporary energy conduit (can be used once to boost spell/damage in future encounter)

- **Partial Success:** Pylon stabilized but minor surges continue; small rewards or partial salvage.

- **Failure:** Pylon remains unstable, minor surges occur, and environmental hazards persist — could tie into Iri’s instability escalating.

## Iri Outcome + Final Encounter

### Core Idea

Iri doesn’t just get “better” or “worse.” Instead, the pylon determines **how her magic manifests**:

- **Success → controlled externalization (separate creature)**

- **Failure → uncontrolled internalization (tied to Iri directly)**

Same theme. Different tone, difficulty, and emotional weight.

### IF THE PARTY SUCCEEDS (Stabilized Pylon)

#### What Happens to Iri

- The moment the final conduit stabilizes, the hum drops into harmony.

- Iri collapses to their knees — breathing hard, but no longer overwhelmed.

- The wild magic **peels away from them**, like mist being drawn out.

**Read-Aloud Beat:**  “The light around the child flickers… then pulls free, unraveling into the air like threads of color being gently unwound.”

👉 The magic doesn’t disappear — it **coalesces nearby**.

#### Final Encounter — “Harmonic Echo”

**Concept:**  A **detached, unstable construct** of Iri’s magic — no longer part of them, but still volatile.

**Visual:**

- Floating, semi-humanoid or amorphous

- Made of shifting prism-light and glass fragments

- Moves in smooth, almost peaceful patterns… until disturbed

#### Behavior

- Not immediately hostile

- Reacts to:

  - Loud noise

  - Aggressive movement

  - Being attacked

👉 Players can:

- Fight it

- Contain it

- Calm it (hard but possible)

#### Stat Concept (CR 1-ish)

**HP:** ~22 **AC:** 12 **Resist:** lightning/force **Vulnerable:** thunder

**Actions:**

- **Prismatic Pulse** — small AoE (DC 11 Dex, 1d6 force)

- **Color Shift (Recharge 5–6)** — gains advantage or imposes disadvantage randomly

#### Twist Mechanic

Players can use:

- **Arcana / Performance / Persuasion (DC 12–13)**  to “harmonize” it instead of killing it

**If calmed instead of killed:**

- It dissolves peacefully

- Leaves behind:

  - **Intact Shard**

  - **Vial of Wild Residue**

- Iri watches → emotional payoff

#### Outcome (Success Path)

- Iri is **stable but still magical**

- No longer in immediate danger

- They may:

  - Show controlled flickers of magic

  - Bond with a PC (especially whoever helped calm them)

👉 Tone: *Hopeful, controlled, mysterious*

### IF THE PARTY FAILS (Unstable Pylon)

#### What Happens to Iri

- The pylon emits a violent harmonic spike.

- Iri screams — and this time, the magic **doesn’t separate**.

**Read-Aloud Beat:**  “The light doesn’t leave them. It surges inward—then explodes outward in a violent pulse.”

- The child becomes the **center of the instability**

#### Final Encounter — “Surgebound Manifestation”

**Concept:**  Iri’s magic **erupts outward violently**, forming a chaotic entity **anchored to them**.

#### Visual

- Larger, more chaotic version of the Echo

- Jagged crystal limbs, snapping arcs of energy

- Tethered to Iri by threads of light

👉 From a distance? **It looks like a small, broken dragon silhouette in flashes of lightning.**  (Perfect callback to your dragon misdirection theme)

#### Behavior

- Aggressive and unstable

- Attacks anything nearby

- BUT:

  - Always returns toward Iri

  - Reacts when Iri is approached

#### Stat Concept (Harder CR 1–2)

**HP:** ~30 **AC:** 13

**Traits:**

- **Tethered Core:**  While within 10 ft of Iri → +2 AC

- **Unstable Form:**  When hit, roll d6:

  - 1–2: lashes attacker (1 lightning)

  - 3–6: normal

**Actions:**

- **Surge Slam** — 1d6+2 lightning/force

- **Wild Burst (Recharge 5–6)** — 10 ft AoE (DC 12 Dex, 1d6, push 5 ft)

#### Critical Twist

Players have **two options**:

### 1. Fight & Destroy It

- Creature collapses violently

- Iri takes **minor backlash** (falls unconscious, but survives)

### 2. Stabilize Iri Mid-Fight (Harder, cooler)

- Skill challenge during combat:

  - Arcana / Persuasion / Medicine (DC 13)

  - 2 successes before 2 failures

👉 On success:

- Creature weakens (loses tether bonus)

- Final blow becomes easier OR it dissipates

#### Outcome (Failure Path)

- Iri survives, but:

  - Exhausted

  - Frightened of their own power

- Magic still unstable — just less explosive

👉 Tone: *Dangerous, unresolved, emotionally heavy*
