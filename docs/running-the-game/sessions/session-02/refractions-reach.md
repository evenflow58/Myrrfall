# Refraction's Reach

### Enter

As you step through the portal, the world shifts to fractured light.

The ground beneath your feet is hard and crystalline — pink glass shot through with glowing blue veins. Sparks of energy flicker beneath the surface like lightning trapped in stone. In the distance, a storm churns against a torn horizon, prismatic arcs striking the wasteland in silent flashes.

But below you, nestled in a shallow basin, stands a town.

Steam rises from metal stacks. Lanternlight glows warm against crystal walls. A ring of pylons hum softly around its edges, and though the storm roils beyond them, it does not strike the settlement directly.

The energy still pulses through the streets — faint, restless.

A dwarf stands near the portal ring, goggles pushed up onto his brow, tools hanging from his belt.

He stares at you.

### The Portal Ring (Arrival Zone)

**What they notice immediately:**

- The portal stands inside a circular platform of crystal and brass inlays.

- Four pylons around it hum faintly.

- Chalk markings on the ground — calculations, revisions, frustration notes.

- A faint smell of ozone and hot metal.

**Interactive Hook:**

- A nearby apprentice asks if they’re “the relief team.”

- A clipboard with stabilization readings — Shardfall Fields slightly unstable.

**Subtle clue DC 15 Investigation:** One harmonic frequency listed on the chalkboard doesn’t match the others. It’s older. Hidden. Precise.
