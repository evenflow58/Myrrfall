# Refraction's Reach

## Overview

Refraction's Reach is the first permanent settlement the party encounters within the Prism Expanse. Built atop crystalline bedrock and protected by a network of resonance pylons, it serves as both a refuge from the Prism Storms and a hub for researchers, engineers, merchants, and explorers.

Although the town appears prosperous, every resident understands that its survival depends upon technology that is beginning to fail.

### Atmosphere

- Industrious
- Hopeful but anxious
- Arcane technology mixed with rugged practicality

## First Impressions

### Sights

- Crystal streets laced with glowing blue veins.
- Brass pipes carrying steam between workshops.
- Resonance pylons surrounding the settlement.
- Lanterns glowing with captured crystal light.
- Engineers and laborers constantly repairing equipment.

### Sounds

- A constant harmonic hum from the pylons.
- Steam vents releasing pressure.
- Metal striking metal from distant workshops.
- Occasional crackles of unstable arcane energy.

### Smells

- Hot brass.
- Ozone.
- Burning crystal dust.
- Machine oil.

---

## Notable Features

### Portal Ring

A circular platform of crystal and brass serves as the town's primary gateway to the outside world.

The ring is surrounded by four resonance pylons that stabilize portal travel and help shield the settlement from nearby Prism Storms.

**Details**

- Chalkboards covered with resonance calculations.
- Tools and spare crystal components scattered nearby.
- Apprentices constantly recording portal readings.
- The smell of ozone lingers in the air.

### Storm Barrier

The resonance pylons surrounding the settlement generate an invisible barrier that weakens incoming Prism Storms before they reach the town.

Residents rarely discuss the possibility of failure—but everyone watches the pylons.

## Discoveries

### Strange Harmonic

**DC 15 Investigation**

Among the resonance calculations, one harmonic frequency differs from every other entry.

Unlike the newer measurements, this one is older, more precise, and written in a different hand.

It appears someone deliberately preserved it rather than erasing it.

**Future Payoff**

This harmonic becomes important later in the campaign when the party begins uncovering the true nature of the Prism Expanse.
