# Glasslight Hearth

**Scenario:**  The party enters the tavern for a warm meal, a drink, or to gather information, but the tavern is in a bit of chaos: lamps flicker, a few tables vibrate, and most importantly, the plumbing isn’t working. No drinks are flowing, the hot sap-water for cooking is delayed, and the staff is frazzled.

**Read-Aloud:**  “As you step inside the Glasslight Hearth, a faint hum of stressed magic hits your ears. Bram Fiddlebranch, the gnome keeper, is muttering over a jumble of pipes that snake through the tavern’s walls and floor. One tap drips colored liquid slowly, the other spurts harmless sparks. Steam curls from a crooked spout near the bar. ‘I swear,’ Bram grumbles, ‘these pipes were fine yesterday… something’s… not right.’”

### Objectives for the Players

The party can attempt to **diagnose and stabilize the magical plumbing** to get the tavern back to normal.

**Mechanic:**

- Success Threshold: 2 successes before 2 failures.

- **Primary Skills:** Arcana (magical energy flow), Nature (resin/wood reactions), Investigation (find blockages/malfunctions), Sleight of Hand (fine adjustments).

- **Support Rolls:** Athletics (hold pipes in place), Perception (detect weak points), Insight (read the “mood” of the plumbing).

**Success Tokens:** Can be used to reroll, stabilize a node instantly, or prevent a small surge from causing chaos.

### Complications on Failure

- Pipes pulse, releasing harmless but chaotic energy: drinks glow, food levitates, tables shake.

- 1d4 magical mishaps:

  1. Steam jet scalds one party member (1d4 fire or force damage).

  1. A floating drink spills over a guest → minor social consequence / NPC complaint.

  1. Sudden wild surge → glasswood trimmings bend unpredictably.

  1. Temporary blackout → magical lamps go dim, next roll at disadvantage.

### Cause of the Malfunction

**Wild Magic Intrusion (Velrix Tie-In)**

- Small pulses of external magical energy (from nearby pylons destabilized by Velrix) are entering the plumbing system.

- Certain pipes glow or pulse in sync with Iri’s latent magic if she’s nearby.

- Investigation DC 12 → see faint energy “resonance lines” along the pipes.

- Arcana DC 13 → identify that the pulses amplify pressure in the sap-water, causing overflow and backflow.

### Possible Outcomes

**Full Success (2 successes before 2 failures):**

- Plumbing is restored. Drinks and food resume flowing.

- Party gains local goodwill / +1 success token toward stabilizing Iri.

- Arcana / Nature rolls can reveal subtle hints about Velrix’s interference in the area.

**Partial Success:**

- Plumbing works, but one tap or pipe remains unpredictable → small wild surge or humorous mishap.

- Party still gains minor information about magical energy flow or Resonance.

**Failure (2 failures before 2 successes):**

- Taverns’ plumbing chaos persists for a while.

- Patrons are upset → potential minor social penalty.

- Could trigger a small mini-surge (flickering lights, floating mugs, mild electrical discharge) → introduces early stakes about wild magic instability.
