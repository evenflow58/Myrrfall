# Echo Crucible: Memory Gate

**Scene Setup:**  As the party’s chosen path reconverges, they arrive at a **tall crystalline archway** spanning the canyon. The surface shimmers with residual spell echoes, twisting and reflecting faint past spell shapes. When they approach, the arch hums, and the air inside **distorts the past — glimpses of Fallen Dawn rituals replay**.

Read-Aloud: “A crystal arch stretches between the canyon walls. Within its frame, shimmering afterimages of spells flicker and twist, like long-forgotten actions trapped in glass. When you step closer, the echoes pulse in rhythm with your magic, and the gate shimmers with a faintly inscribed riddle: *‘Only by aligning what was with what is, may you step through time unbroken.’*”

## Puzzle Mechanics

**Objective:** Unlock the Memory Gate by **matching echoes to their origin spells**.

1. **The Gate has 4 Echo Panels**: Each panel is a floating crystal plaque.

1. **Each Panel Shows a Distorted Spell Echo**:

  - Fireball residue

  - Shielding ward

  - Lightning bolt

  - Minor conjuration (floating object)

1. **Players must “restore” the echoes** by reproducing the correct type of magic (or mimicking its effect) to align the panel.

  - Can use **Arcana, Performance, Sleight of Hand, Nature, or even spellcasting** to interact.

### Skill Challenges

**Each Panel: DC 12–14** (adjust by party level).

- **Arcana DC 12:** Identify what the echo actually represents.

- **Arcana DC 13 / Spellcasting:** Reproduce the effect via a spell, cantrip, or creatively described action.

- **Performance / Sleight of Hand DC 12:** Physically manipulate crystal in rhythm with the echo.

- **Nature / Insight DC 11:** Sense the natural “flow” of magic and mimic it without spells.

**Optional Teamwork:**

- Two or more characters can combine checks for advantage (encourages creative collaboration).

#### Consequences

| Result | Description | Roleplay / Story Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Panels Correct | Gate opens, shimmering crystal steps inside reveal the MacGuffin beyond. | The party sees echoes of Fallen Dawn’s failed stabilizations. The MacGuffin glows faintly. Iri’s wild magic stabilizes slightly if present. |
| 1–2 Panels Wrong | Gate partially opens; echoes surge outward. | Small wild surges erupt, objects levitate or crackle, forcing a quick mini skill save (Dex or Arcana DC 12). Salvageable Bottled Surge or Residual Crystal may appear. |
| All Panels Wrong | Gate locks; echoes backlash violently. | Iri (if nearby) may summon a minor shard construct or unstable magical effect (AC 11, HP 10, 1d6 damage attacks) that must be mollified or avoided. Party can retry after a short rest. |

#### Environmental / Roleplay Cues

- **Visual:** Each panel shows a faint replay of a past spell.

- **Audio:** Whispering echoes of incantations.

- **Tactile:** Crystals resonate when touched; incorrect “force” pulses lightly back.

- **Iri Interaction:** If present, the child’s magic may make the echoes more volatile (adds urgency or flavor to failure).
