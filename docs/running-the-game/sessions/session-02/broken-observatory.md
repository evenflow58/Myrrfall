# The Broken Observatory

**Theme:** Fallen Dawn Secrets

Ruins of a spired arcane observation tower built around a floating crystal array.

Half collapsed. Half phased out of reality.

### Scenario:

- Gravity shifts inside.

- Rooms overlap temporally.

- The party meets a magical afterimage of a Fallen Dawn member mid-research.

### Lore:

- They were trying to correct a “Prismatic Convergence Event.”

- A being or object at the center of the Expanse refracts reality.

- It may not be malicious.
