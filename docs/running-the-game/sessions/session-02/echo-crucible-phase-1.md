# Echo Crucible: Arrival and Orientation

## Read-Aloud Expansion

“As you move deeper into the canyon, your reflections don’t quite match your movements. A step echoes half a second too late. A shadow lifts its arm before you do. Then—faintly—you see figures walking the canyon ahead of you… repeating the same path, over and over.”

### What They Encounter

#### 1. Echo Trails (Navigation Clue)

- Ghostly figures walking a **repeating route through the canyon**

- They:

  - Avoid certain areas

  - Step in specific places

  - Pause at junctions

👉 This is your **breadcrumb trail**

**Player Discovery**

- **Insight / Investigation DC 11–12**  → These figures are not random — they’re following a *safe path*

#### 2. First Echo Trigger (Safe Introduction)

- A small magical effect replays nearby:

  - A flickering firebolt

  - A shield snapping into place

- No danger — just visual

👉 Teaches:

- Magic is being *replayed*, not cast

#### Goal of Phase 1

Players learn:

- The canyon has **patterns**

- Following echoes = safer movement

- Magic behaves *strangely* here
