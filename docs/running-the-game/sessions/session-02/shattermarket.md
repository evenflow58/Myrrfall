# The Shattermarket

The Shattermarket is a scatter of crystal-framed stalls clustered along a sloping street, their awnings stitched from canvas and reflective foil.

Lanternlight refracts through hanging shards, casting shifting rainbows across the ground. Trinkets hum softly on velvet cushions — rings that flicker with trapped light, glass baubles swirling with mist, jagged fragments labeled in hurried ink.

Vendors call out cautiously, never too loud.

Beyond the stalls, the storm’s colors ripple faintly across every polished surface, as if the market itself is listening.

**Vibe:**  Cautious optimism. Traders who profit from instability.

## General Rule for Purchases

Nothing here should:

- Outshine class abilities

- Replace core magic items

- Grant permanent stat boosts

- Be fully reliable

Most items are:

- 1/day

- 3 charges

- Risk/reward

- Situationally useful in the Expanse

Organized by Vendor Type

#### Coil Salvager’s Stall (Utility + Repair)

**Vendor: Brakka Ironthumb**  Gruff half-orc missing two fingers, sells cracked tech

##### 1. Surge-Safe Gloves

*Cost:* 1 Stabilization Shard or 40g Once per long rest, ignore damage from a wild surge effect triggered by touching an object.

##### 2. Pylon Spanner

*Cost:* 1 Damaged Pylon Component Advantage on one check to repair or stabilize a magical device. Breaks after 3 uses.

##### 3. Crystal Patch Kit

*Cost:* 2 Prismglass Fragments As an action, temporarily mend cracked crystal terrain to create stable footing for 1 minute.

#### Storm-Touched Curios

**Vendor: Velrix Dorne** (yes, him)

He sells “experimental” goods.

##### 4. Bottled Surge

*Cost:* 1 vial of wild residue or 50g Throw up to 30 ft. Roll d6: 1–2: 1d6 force burst (5 ft) 3–4: Difficult terrain (crystalline growth) 5: Teleports target 10 ft randomly 6: Grants target advantage on next attack

##### 5. Resonance Chime

*Cost:* 1 intact shard Ring to detect active pylon instability within 120 ft. Hum grows louder as danger increases.

##### 6. Fractured Lens

*Cost:* Barter only (valuable salvage) Look through it to see magical stress lines in objects or creatures. 1/day advantage on Arcana or Investigation in magical zones. On a 1, user suffers minor surge effect.

#### Fringe Traders (Weird but Harmless)

##### 7. Prism Jerky

*Cost:* 5g or trade Eat to gain resistance to lightning OR force damage for 1 round. Must roll Con save (DC 10) or glow faintly for 1 hour.

##### 8. Echo Chalk

*Cost:* 10g Draw a symbol. It repeats the last loud sound made in that space when stepped on.

##### 9. Stormline Compass

*Cost:* 1 salvage + 20g Points toward the nearest magical disturbance instead of north.

#### For Kular Specifically

Items that resonate with his theme but aren’t broken.

##### 10. Harmonic Stabilizer Pin

*Cost:* 1 Pylon Component Attach to armor or tool. Once per day reduce surge damage by 1d4.

##### 11. Calibrated Flux Gauge

*Cost:* Trade schematic or research notes Shows whether an area is:

- Stable

- Building

- On verge of rupture

Does not predict when.

**Skylar Hook:**  One vendor has a shard shaped vaguely like a curved talon. When Skylar approaches:

- The shard vibrates faintly.

- The storm flickers in the distance.

- The vendor insists it was found near Shardfall Fields after a “wing-shaped lightning strike.”

Not proof of dragons. Just suggestion.

### Velrix “Three-Fingers” Nahal — Relic Broker

**Race:** Human **Energy:** Cheerful, predatory, smooth **Role:** Artifact dealer who knows too much

Missing two fingers on one hand — lost to a “minor instability event.”

He exaggerates constantly — but not always.

When Skylar approaches: One curved shard trembles faintly. Velrix notices immediately.

“Oh. That one doesn’t do that for most people.”

He offers it for a steep price — or a favor.

He claims:

“Lightning out there doesn’t strike randomly. Sometimes it shapes itself.”

Is he lying? Maybe.

Is he entirely wrong? No.

### Sabotage

Velrix inserted a **Resonance Catalyst Shard** into the Shardfall pylon core.

The shard:

- Slightly amplified instability.

- Drew surge energy toward the pylon.

- Was meant to “stress test” the system.

He wanted to see:

- If the pylons suppress something trying to form.

- If a dragon-storm manifestation could stabilize itself.

He miscalculated the feedback loop.

When Rhoric’s team attempted recalibration, the catalyst caused:

- A harmonic snap.

- A crystal inversion surge.

- A localized storm implosion.

The team wasn’t incinerated.

They were displaced.

Possibly phased. Possibly scattered across the Expanse. Possibly fused with wild magic phenomena.
