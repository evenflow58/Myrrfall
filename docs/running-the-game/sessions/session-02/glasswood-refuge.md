# The Glasswood Refuge

**Theme:** People Adapting to Chaos

A small settlement built from translucent, magically hardened trees.

The inhabitants harvest prism-glass sap and survive by predicting magic fluctuations.

### Mini Event: Shaping the Glasswood

**Location:** Sapglass Workshop **NPC Host:** Mira Quillspindle (gnome artificer)

This event can happen while the party is waiting for news about the unstable child, or simply while exploring the refuge.

#### Read-Aloud Introduction

Inside the workshop, the air smells faintly of sap and hot resin. Long planks of translucent glasswood rest on heavy work tables, their inner veins glowing faintly like trapped lightning.

A gnome with soot-streaked goggles looks up as you enter.

“Well! Fresh hands. Good timing.”

She taps a glowing plank with a small hammer and it hums like a tuning fork.

“Glasswood doesn’t shape itself. Care to help before this one decides to explode?”

#### CORE MECHANIC — SUCCESS TOKENS

#### Each step has:

#### 1 Primary Roll

#### Any number of Support Actions (Different Angles)

##### Primary Roll

#### DC 12

#### On success → Step completed

##### Support (Different Angles)

#### Players roll relevant skills (Arcana, Investigation, Nature, etc.)

On Success gain 1 Success Token

On Failure trigger a Complication

#### SUCCESS TOKENS (Party Pool)

#### Tokens can be spent at any time to:

#### +2 to a roll

#### Reroll a die

#### Cancel a complication

#### Add +2 to the final crafting result

#### 👉 Max 3 tokens held at once (keeps it tight and game-y)

#### STEP 1 — Stabilize the Wood

**Primary Skills:** Nature, Arcana, Medicine The glasswood pulses erratically, light flaring and dimming.

**Flavor:**  The branch twitches in your hands, its glow spiking unpredictably.

**Support Ideas:**

- Arcana → dampen wild magic

- Medicine → “treat” it like a living thing

- Insight → read its rhythm

**Failure Complication:**

- A pulse of energy lashes out → All players take 1 damage OR next roll is at disadvantage

#### STEP 2 — Shape the Form

**Primary Skills:** Athletics, Sleight of Hand, Survival The wood resists rigid shaping—it wants to curve, twist, adapt.

**Flavor:**  It bends where it shouldn’t… and refuses where it should.

**Support Ideas:**

- Investigation → find natural grain lines

- Perception → anticipate shifts

- Arcana → soften structure briefly

**Failure Complication:**

- The structure cracks → Lose 1 Success Token OR next step DC increases to 13

#### STEP 3 — Set the Light

**Primary Skills:** Arcana, Performance, Persuasion The final step: convincing the glasswood to *hold* its shape and glow.

**Flavor:**  The light inside flickers wildly—like it’s deciding what it wants to become.

**Support Ideas:**

- Performance → guide the rhythm

- Persuasion → “encourage” the magic

- Religion → invoke harmony/order

**Failure Complication:**

- Wild surge → Roll 1d4:

1. Flash blind (disadvantage next roll)

1. Sudden heat (1 damage)

1. Object warps (reduced reward)

1. Light escapes (lose 1 token)

#### OUTCOMES

#### 3 Successes (Clean Craft)

- Gain **Refined Glasswood Item**

- Gain **1–2 Glasswood Materials** (for barter)

- NPC impressed

#### 2 Successes (Imperfect Craft)

- Gain **Unstable Glasswood Item**

- Gain **1 Material**

#### 1 or Fewer Successes

- Warped or failed item

- Still gain **1 Material** (they learn something)

#### SAMPLE REWARDS

**Refined Glasswood Wand/Core**

- Once per day: add +1 to a spell roll or save DC

**Glasswood Bracer**

- Once per day: reduce force/lightning damage by 2

**Raw Glasswood**

- Valuable in Shattermarket for barter

#### NPC: Craft Guide

**Name:** Lysa Vant Quiet, sharp-eyed, always watching the wood more than the people.

**Flavor Line:**  “Most folk try to force it. That’s why most folk fail.”

**Secret Read:**  She subtly favors groups that **work together** over individuals who dominate.

#### Bonus Roleplay Moments

During crafting Mira might mention:

- Glasswood only grows where **wild magic breathes**

- The grove has been **behaving strangely lately**

- The pylon has needed **constant recalibration**

This foreshadows the unstable child event.
