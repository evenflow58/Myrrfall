# The Lantern Archive

The Lantern Archive stands quiet and square against the shifting horizon, its walls reinforced with crystal ribs and iron bands.

Inside, soft golden lamps hang from delicate chains, their light steady despite the faint hum in the air. Maps of the Expanse cover one wall, pinned with colored markers and frantic annotations. Shelves bow under stacks of journals, loose schematics, and carefully wrapped crystal samples.

The room smells of ink, dust, and charged stone.

This is where people try to make sense of what refuses to be understood.

**Inside:**

- Maps of the Expanse with marked volatile zones.

- Diagrams of stabilization networks.

- Early sketches labeled “Fallen Dawn Initiative.”

**Lore Beat:**  [The Prism Expanse](../../../lore/world/prism-expanse.md) was not always like this. There was a surge event decades ago. After that, the first pylons were installed.

No record of *why* the surge happened.

Just that it did.

**Race:** Half-Elf **Energy:** Calm, tired, too observant **Role:** Historian who knows more than she publishes

Pel is the type who speaks gently but remembers everything.

She has records of:

- The first surge event

- Early Fallen Dawn involvement

- Funding sources that don’t match public accounts

She doesn’t accuse anyone.

But she does say:

“The Expanse wasn’t born. It was made… or unmade.”

She notices:

- Thorn doesn’t appear in old resonance logs (warforged weren’t common).

- Skylar’s magic matches early surge descriptions.

She may quietly give them a redacted journal entry.
