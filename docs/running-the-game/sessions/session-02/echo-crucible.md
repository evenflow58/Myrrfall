# The Echo Crucible

### Arrival — Read Aloud

“The land splits open into a narrow crystalline canyon, its walls rising in jagged, mirrored planes of pink and violet glass. Light fractures, repeats, and lingers. As you step inside, a faint shimmer ripples through the air… and for a moment, you swear you see movement that isn’t yours.”

### Core Mechanic — Echo Casting

**Any spell cast in the Crucible risks triggering an Echo.**

#### When a spell is cast:

Roll **1d6**:

| d6 | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | No effect |
| 4–5 | Minor Echo — a visual/audio replay of a past spell |
| 6 | Full Echo Manifestation — a past spell partially recreates itself |

#### What Echoes Look Like

- Ghostly silhouettes of past casters

- Spell effects replaying slightly out of sync

- Magic that *almost* interacts with reality

👉 Important: These are **not illusions** — they are **residual magical imprints**

#### Core Navigation Idea: Follow the Strongest Memory

The party doesn’t get directions from a person. They learn to navigate by noticing:

- **Echo Density** → more echoes = closer to the Core

- **Stability Patterns** → stable areas = safe paths

- **Repetition** → certain movements or spell traces repeat, forming “paths”

👉 The canyon *guides them* through memory.

#### PHASE 1 — Arrival & Orientation (20–30 min)

##### Read-Aloud Expansion

“As you move deeper into the canyon, your reflections don’t quite match your movements. A step echoes half a second too late. A shadow lifts its arm before you do. Then—faintly—you see figures walking the canyon ahead of you… repeating the same path, over and over.”

##### What They Encounter

###### 1. Echo Trails (Navigation Clue)

- Ghostly figures walking a **repeating route through the canyon**

- They:

  - Avoid certain areas

  - Step in specific places

  - Pause at junctions

👉 This is your **breadcrumb trail**

**Player Discovery**

- **Insight / Investigation DC 11–12**  → These figures are not random — they’re following a *safe path*

###### 2. First Echo Trigger (Safe Introduction)

- A small magical effect replays nearby:

  - A flickering firebolt

  - A shield snapping into place

- No danger — just visual

👉 Teaches:

- Magic is being *replayed*, not cast

###### Goal of Phase 1

Players learn:

- The canyon has **patterns**

- Following echoes = safer movement

- Magic behaves *strangely* here

#### PHASE 2 — Interaction & Experimentation (45–60 min)

Now they start **triggering the system**.

###### Area: The Split Path

The canyon divides into 2–3 routes.

**Environmental Clues:**

- One path:

  - Heavy echo activity

  - Flickering, unstable

- One path:

  - Calm, faint echoes

- One path:

  - Broken reflections, erratic timing

👉 No “wrong” path — just different experiences

###### What the Party Actually Sees

When they arrive:

**Read-Aloud:**  “The canyon narrows, then splits into three diverging paths. Each one reflects light differently—one flickers with constant motion, one is eerily still, and one fractures your reflection into jagged, delayed pieces. Faint figures move through one of the paths… repeating the same steps over and over.”

###### The Three Paths (Clear Differences)

**The Flickering Path (Unstable / Active)**

**Visual:**

- Constant spell echoes firing

- Firebolts, shields, arcane flashes repeating

- Loud, chaotic

**What it means:**  👉 High echo activity = closer to raw magic

**Gameplay:**

- More frequent Echo triggers

- Easier to understand how echoes work

- Slightly more dangerous

**What players learn:**

“Magic here repeats whether we want it to or not.”

**The Still Path (Stable / Faint)**

**Visual:**

- Very few echoes

- Quiet, almost normal

- Subtle glow in the walls

**What it means:**  👉 Low activity = safer, but less informative

**Gameplay:**

- Fewer hazards

- Slower progression

- Fewer clues about the Core

**What players learn:**

“Some areas resist the effect… but don’t show us much.”

**The Fractured Path (Distorted / Wrong)**

**Visual:**

- Reflections don’t match movement

- Echoes are out of sync (before/after actions)

- Time feels “off”

**What it means:**  👉 This path is **misaligned memory**

**Gameplay:**

- Weird effects:

  - Delayed actions

  - Echoes triggering early/late

- Trickier navigation

**What players learn:**

“Something here is broken—not just unstable.”

###### How Players Choose (Without You Explaining)

Give them **these clues**:

**Perception / Investigation DC 11–12**

- Flickering Path: “High magical activity”

- Still Path: “Less interference”

- Fractured Path: “Something feels wrong, not just unstable”

**Insight DC 11**

- The repeating figures are using **one specific path**  👉 That’s your **subtle ‘safe’ route hint**

###### The Key: They’re All Valid Choices

No matter what they pick:

- They still reach the Core

- They just experience **different encounters along the way**

###### What Happens After They Choose

Each path leads to **one encounter + convergence**

**Example Flow:**

| Path | Encounter |
| --- | --- |
| Flickering | Echo Feedback Zone (spell chaos) |
| Still | Memory Gate (puzzle, repetition) |
| Fractured | Distortion Zone (timing weirdness) |

👉 After 1–2 encounters: **All paths reconnect**

###### Simple Mental Model (For You as DM)

Think of it like:

“Three doors → different rooms → same hallway after”

You’re not building 3 full dungeons. Just:

- 1 themed encounter per path

- Then merge back into main progression

#### PHASE 3 — Memory Gate Puzzle

**Scene Setup:**  As the party’s chosen path reconverges, they arrive at a **tall crystalline archway** spanning the canyon. The surface shimmers with residual spell echoes, twisting and reflecting faint past spell shapes. When they approach, the arch hums, and the air inside **distorts the past — glimpses of Fallen Dawn rituals replay**.

Read-Aloud: “A crystal arch stretches between the canyon walls. Within its frame, shimmering afterimages of spells flicker and twist, like long-forgotten actions trapped in glass. When you step closer, the echoes pulse in rhythm with your magic, and the gate shimmers with a faintly inscribed riddle: *‘Only by aligning what was with what is, may you step through time unbroken.’*”

###### Puzzle Mechanics

**Objective:** Unlock the Memory Gate by **matching echoes to their origin spells**.

1. **The Gate has 4 Echo Panels**: Each panel is a floating crystal plaque.

1. **Each Panel Shows a Distorted Spell Echo**:

  - Fireball residue

  - Shielding ward

  - Lightning bolt

  - Minor conjuration (floating object)

1. **Players must “restore” the echoes** by reproducing the correct type of magic (or mimicking its effect) to align the panel.

  - Can use **Arcana, Performance, Sleight of Hand, Nature, or even spellcasting** to interact.

###### Skill Challenges

**Each Panel: DC 12–14** (adjust by party level).

- **Arcana DC 12:** Identify what the echo actually represents.

- **Arcana DC 13 / Spellcasting:** Reproduce the effect via a spell, cantrip, or creatively described action.

- **Performance / Sleight of Hand DC 12:** Physically manipulate crystal in rhythm with the echo.

- **Nature / Insight DC 11:** Sense the natural “flow” of magic and mimic it without spells.

**Optional Teamwork:**

- Two or more characters can combine checks for advantage (encourages creative collaboration).

###### Consequences

| Result | Description | Roleplay / Story Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Panels Correct | Gate opens, shimmering crystal steps inside reveal the MacGuffin beyond. | The party sees echoes of Fallen Dawn’s failed stabilizations. The MacGuffin glows faintly. Iri’s wild magic stabilizes slightly if present. |
| 1–2 Panels Wrong | Gate partially opens; echoes surge outward. | Small wild surges erupt, objects levitate or crackle, forcing a quick mini skill save (Dex or Arcana DC 12). Salvageable Bottled Surge or Residual Crystal may appear. |
| All Panels Wrong | Gate locks; echoes backlash violently. | Iri (if nearby) may summon a minor shard construct or unstable magical effect (AC 11, HP 10, 1d6 damage attacks) that must be mollified or avoided. Party can retry after a short rest. |

###### Environmental / Roleplay Cues

- **Visual:** Each panel shows a faint replay of a past spell.

- **Audio:** Whispering echoes of incantations.

- **Tactile:** Crystals resonate when touched; incorrect “force” pulses lightly back.

- **Iri Interaction:** If present, the child’s magic may make the echoes more volatile (adds urgency or flavor to failure).

#### Core Encounter

**Scene Setup:**  After passing the Memory Gate, the party enters a **wide crystalline cavern**, the walls pulsing with refracted light and residual spell echoes. At the center lies a **floating crystalline Core**, fractured and vibrating with unstable magical energy. Sparks of residual magic leap from it, forming ghostly echoes of past spellcasters’ attempts to stabilize it.

Read-Aloud: “The cavern stretches wide, its walls alive with flickering light. At its center floats a jagged crystal sphere, spinning and cracking in unstable harmony. Magical echoes pulse outward, and every step closer sends tiny shards leaping from the floor. This… is the heart of the Echo Crucible.”

##### Encounter Design

**Core Mechanics:**

1. **The Core:**

  - AC: 15

  - HP: 60 (adjust for party level)

  - Vulnerable to stabilization skills: Arcana, Tinkering, Nature, or even spell-based dampening.

  - Every turn the Core pulses (roll 1d4) — small wild surge effects happen: damage, area disruption, or temporary chaos.

1. **Add Waves (Echo Constructs):**

  - Every 2 rounds, 1d3 minor shard or elemental creatures spawn near the Core.

  - AC 12, HP 10, small melee or ranged attacks (1d6 damage).

  - They are semi-hostile echoes, tied to past spell failures, but **non-lethal if the party wants to avoid them**.

  - Optional: allow Arcana/Performance/Nature checks to **dampen/add energy to slow or redirect them**.

##### Turn Structure

**Each Round:**

1. **Core Pulse:** Roll 1d4

  - 1: Small shockwave — everyone within 10 ft must make Dex DC 12 or fall prone.

  - 2: Residual magic surge — random 5-ft square becomes hazardous (1d4 damage or difficult terrain).

  - 3: Pulse of echoes — creates phantom visual distractions; Perception DC 12 to avoid disadvantage on attack or skill check.

  - 4: Unstable burst — Arcana DC 12 or Nature DC 12 to stabilize the Core before the next round, or the Core loses 1 stability point (adds to difficulty).

1. **Add Spawns:** 1d3 minor enemies enter the field near the Core.

1. **Player Actions:**

  - **Core Stabilizers:** Use Arcana, Tinkering, Nature, Spellcasting, or creative solutions to stabilize energy.

    - Each success: 1 stability token.

    - **Goal:** 6 stability tokens to fully stabilize the Core.

  - **Fighters / Defenders:** Deal with adds to protect stabilizers or slow enemy approach.

### 🏆 Victory & Salvage

**If Core Stabilized:**

- Magical echoes calm. Light refracts harmoniously.

- **Salvage drops:**

  - **Calibrated Flux Gauge (trade / schematic)**

  - **Stormline Compass (1 salvage)**

  - Minor Prismglass Fragments for Shattermarket trade

**If Core Partially Stabilized:**

- Core hums but still pulses irregularly; adds stop spawning, but residual magic causes ongoing minor hazards.

- **Salvage drops:**

  - Bottled Surge (1 vial)

  - Unstable Glasswood fragment

**If Core Fails:**

- Core erupts violently; small crystal shards shoot out (1d6 damage), room partially damaged.

- Iri’s wild magic flares, creating a temporary **magical hazard** or minor creature (like a shard elemental).

- Party can retreat or try a risky “catch-up stabilization” with a skill challenge.
