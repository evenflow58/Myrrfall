# The Shardfall Fields

### Core Identity

A wide basin of fractured pink crystal plains where lightning never fully stops.

At the center: A broken stabilization pylon. Above it: a storm vortex that does not move.

Something circles inside the storm.

### Read-Aloud (Arrival at Edge of Fields)

The crystalline ground slopes downward into a wide basin of shattered glass-like terrain. Blue-white lightning arcs between jagged spires, never fully touching the ground—always hovering just above it.

At the center of the basin stands a tall, fractured pylon, its upper rings twisted and sparking.

The storm above it does not drift.

It rotates.

And something moves within it.

### 🌩 Environmental Mechanics — The Storm

This isn’t constant damage. It’s pressure.

#### Storm Pulse (Every Round in Open Basin)

Roll 1d6 at initiative 20:

1–2: Static Charge — next metal weapon attack deals +1 lightning (also to wielder on nat 1) 3–4: Crackling Wind — ranged attacks at disadvantage this round 5: Lightning Fork — random 5-ft square struck (Dex DC 11, 1d6 lightning) 6: Storm Surge — creature airborne or climbing must make DC 10 Str or be pushed 10 ft

Encourages movement, cover, and tactical thinking.

### 🐉 The Stormbound “Dragon”

You don’t want a real dragon. You want misdirection.

#### Stormveil Drifter (Cloaker Variant)

Reskinned cloaker — but scaled for 4 level 1 PCs.

Visually:

- Manta-like shape

- Crystalline ridges along wings

- Glowing blue veins

- Long trailing tail filaments

- Dragon-like silhouette in lightning flashes

From below in storm silhouette? It absolutely looks like a dragon.

### Stormveil Drifter (CR 1)

AC 13 HP 27 Speed 10 ft, Fly 40 ft

Resist lightning Vulnerable thunder

#### Actions

**Lightning Snap (Melee)**  +4 to hit, 1d6 + 2 lightning

**Storm Cloak (Recharge 5–6)**  Wings flare. 10-ft radius. Creatures DC 11 Con save or blinded until end of next turn (static distortion).

**Vortex Guard**  While within 20 ft of broken pylon, gains +2 AC.

#### Behavior

It is enraged by the pylon

It attacks only if:

- PCs approach pylon aggressively

- They damage it

- They attempt to dismantle pylon

If they observe carefully (DC 12 Insight or Nature):

It coils protectively around the broken pylon during strong lightning pulses.

### 🛠 The Broken Pylon

Description:

The upper focusing ring has been removed and poorly reattached. Crystal rods are misaligned. The base hums irregularly.

Kular immediately knows: This was tampered with.

#### Repair Skill Challenge (3 Successes Before 2 Failures)

Possible checks:

Arcana (realign energy flow) Tinker’s Tools (stabilize crystal mountings) Athletics (hold sparking components steady) Nature (read storm rhythm timing)

Each round repair attempt: Roll storm pulse first.

If they repair while Drifter lives: It becomes increasingly agitated.

#### 🔎 Evidence of Velrix

Clue 1: One of the removed crystal rings is stamped with Coil Yard insignia.

Clue 2: Arcana DC 12 reveals micro-fracture scoring consistent with deliberate destabilization.

Clue 3: A salvage crate partially buried nearby labeled: “Calibration shipment — V.D.”

Not subtle if investigated.

### 💰 Barter Salvage

Scattered through field:

#### Twin-Tone Shard

Hums in two frequencies. Highly valuable in Shattermarket.

#### Charged Pylon Core Fragment

1-use battery for magical item.

#### Storm-Shed Scale

If Drifter survives and leaves peacefully, one wing-scale drops. Counts as rare salvage.

### Lost Team Echo — Shardfall Fields (Revised)

#### Trigger

When a character:

- Approaches within **30 ft of the broken pylon**

- Touches the cracked crystal ring

- Begins examining the damage

The storm spikes violently.

#### Read-Aloud

The air suddenly grows charged, the smell of ozone thick enough to taste.

Lightning lashes across the basin in rapid bursts, illuminating the crystal spires like jagged mirrors.

Then shapes appear in the storm.

Three figures in field gear flicker into view near the pylon, their forms translucent and unstable—as if the storm itself remembers them.

You hear voices carried on the wind.

“Hold the ring steady!” one shouts.

Another voice answers, strained. “The resonance is climbing—this isn’t in the calibration!”

Above them, something vast circles through the clouds.

And then it screams.

### What the Echo Shows

Rhoric’s team is attempting to stabilize the pylon.

### Rhoric

Holding the crystal ring.

“Someone increased the gain threshold—this will pull the storm in!”

### Vale (engineer)

“That’s impossible! That change wasn’t in the design logs!”

### Elra (scout)

Looking upward.

“Whatever that thing is—it’s getting closer!”

Lightning flashes.

For a brief moment the party sees the **Stormveil Drifter**, gliding through the storm.

But it’s different than now.

It’s calm.

Circling curiously.

Then Rhoric wedges a strange crystal into the pylon to stabilize it.

The storm instantly spikes.

The Drifter shrieks in pain.

Lightning detonates across the basin.

The echo fractures and vanishes.

### What Actually Happened

Velrix’s **resonance catalyst shard** caused the pylon to emit an unstable harmonic pulse.

That pulse **agitated the Stormveil Drifter**, whose biology is tuned to storm resonance.

To the creature, the pylon agitates it

So it stays near it, attacking anything that approaches.

Not out of malice.

Out of **rage and confusion**.

### Player Discoveries

### Arcana / Nature (DC 12)

The broken crystal is emitting a **harmonic pulse**.

It’s basically broadcasting magical noise into the storm.

That noise would absolutely enrage storm-adapted creatures.

### Insight (DC 11)

The Drifter’s attacks seem focused on:

- The pylon

- Anyone touching it

Not random aggression.

### Repair Discovery

If the party begins repairing the pylon:

The Drifter becomes **more aggressive initially**.

But once the final repair success happens:

The storm quiets.

The Drifter stops attacking.

It circles once, confused… then glides away into the storm.

### Reward for Non-Lethal Resolution

If the party calms the storm instead of killing it:

When the creature leaves, a **shed crystal scale** falls.

### Stormveil Scale

Barter value in Shattermarket.

Or can be used once to:

- Absorb 1 lightning damage source

- Or grant advantage on one storm-related check

Nothing game-breaking but flavorful.

### Physical Evidence After Echo

Half buried near the pylon is a **supply crate**.

Stamped:

Coil Yard Calibration Shipment Authorized by V. Dorne

Velrix.

### ⚙️ Kular Hook

During repair:

He recognizes calibration method as outdated.

Someone intentionally destabilized the storm containment threshold.

This wasn’t natural degradation.

It was tuned to fail slowly.

### ⏳ Velrix Escalation

If party clears Shardfall quickly:

Velrix remains weak.

If they ignore clues:

Storm intensity increases in future zones.

Drifter returns later stronger.

### 🌈 Extra Layer (Optional)

At basin edge:

Crystallized skeleton of a large reptilian creature fused into terrain.

It is not a dragon.

But clearly something tried to channel dragon-shaped magic here long ago.

Skylar may misinterpret.
