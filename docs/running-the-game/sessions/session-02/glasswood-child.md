# The Glasswood Child

You want this to *interrupt normal play*, not feel like a quest giver.

### Read Aloud (Trigger Moment)

A sharp crack splits the air—like glass snapping under pressure.

Somewhere nearby, a child screams.

A pulse of prismatic light flares between the buildings, briefly painting everything in shifting color. Tools rattle. A lantern bursts.

Then—silence, except for the low hum of unstable magic.

### THE CHILD

**Name:** Iri Vale (or similar—soft, grounded name)

**What’s happening:**

- Wild magic is **manifesting uncontrollably**

- Glasswood nearby is reacting to them

- They are scared, not dangerous (yet)

### FIRST SCENE (Immediate Interaction)

The party finds:

- Iri curled near a warped glasswood structure

- Light flickering across their skin in pulses

- A caretaker trying (and failing) to calm them

### What Players See:

- Objects subtly shifting (colors, shape, gravity flickers)

- Glasswood bending toward the child

- Magic responding to **emotion spikes**

### WHAT’S ACTUALLY GOING ON

- The child is **naturally sensitive to wild magic**

- This happens occasionally in the Expanse

👉 BUT:

Velrix has **destabilized a nearby pylon**, causing:

- Magic surges to spike unpredictably

- Natural “gift” → dangerous overload

### PLAYER OPTIONS (Let Them Drive)

Players can:

- Calm the child (Persuasion / Insight)

- Suppress magic (Arcana)

- Physically stabilize environment

- Observe patterns (Investigation)

### QUICK SKILL MOMENT (Mini, Not Full Event)

Goal: Prevent a surge

- 2 successes before 2 failures (DC 12)

- On fail → small wild surge (visual, not deadly)

👉 This reinforces:

- Magic is unstable

- This is bigger than one moment

### THE LEAD-IN TO THE QUEST

After stabilizing things temporarily:

### Caretaker (or local NPC) explains:

This *just started getting worse recently.*

Before:

- Episodes were small

- Manageable

Now:

- Stronger

- More frequent

- Glasswood reacting violently

### KEY CLUE (VELRIX TIE-IN)

Someone mentions:

“The eastern pylon’s been acting strange… flickering. We sent a team to check it.”

### THE QUEST HOOK

### “Stabilize the Source”

The child doesn’t need curing.

They need:

- The **magic pressure reduced**

### Solution:

Repair / stabilize the **Shardfall-adjacent pylon**

### ADDING EMOTIONAL WEIGHT

Before they leave:

### The child asks:

“Is it going to keep happening?”

No correct answer.

This reinforces:

- Stakes are ongoing

- Fixing the pylon matters

### OPTIONAL TWIST (Highly Recommended)

During the scene, a **small anomaly appears**:

- A shard of crystal hovering near the child

- It pulses in sync with them

If examined:

- It bears a faint **geometric scoring pattern**

👉 This is Velrix’s influence:

- Artificial interference with natural magic flow

### VELRIX CONNECTION (SUBTLE, EARLY)

What the party can learn:

- Something is **amplifying instability**

- It’s **not natural**

- It’s tied to **pylon interference**

NOT:

- Who Velrix is

- Why he’s doing it
