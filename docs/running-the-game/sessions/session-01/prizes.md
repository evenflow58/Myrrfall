# Prize Redemption

## How Tickets Work (DM Guidance)

- Each successful carnival game earns **1-2 tickets**

- Exceptional success, clever roleplay, or creative solutions can earn bonus tickets

- Tickets are **festival-only** but prizes carry forward into the campaign

- Prizes skew toward **utility, flavor, and light combat impact**

### 🟢 Common Prizes (1–2 Tickets)

Fun, flavorful items that add character and small utility.

**1 Ticket**

- **Festival Treat Pack**  A bundle of spiced pastries and sweets. Once consumed, gain advantage on one Charisma check made during the festival.

- **Heroic Badge**  A small pin bearing a hero’s symbol. NPCs recognize it during the festival and react more favorably.

- **Glowthread Scarf**  Glows faintly in dim light; can be extinguished at will.

**2 Tickets**

- **Firecracker Charm**  Once per long rest, create a harmless but loud burst of light and sound (like *thaumaturgy*).

- **Verdant Seed Pouch**  Once planted, instantly grows a small patch of harmless foliage (difficult terrain for 1 round).

- **Whisper Coin**  Flip it to send a whispered message (10 words) to someone you can see within 30 feet.

### 🔵 Uncommon Prizes (3–5 Tickets)

Items with lasting utility and light mechanical impact.

**3 Tickets**

- **Strategist’s Token**  Once per day, add +1 to an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check after seeing the roll.

- **Mask of Festival Faces**  Change your facial features slightly (cosmetic only) for 1 hour.

- **Carnival Healer’s Kit**  Once per long rest, restore 1d6 + proficiency bonus HP when used.

**4 Tickets**

- **Emberglass Vial**  As a reaction once per long rest, reduce fire damage taken by half.

- **Bramblegrip Gloves**  Gain advantage on one Athletics or Acrobatics check per long rest.

- **Jester’s Deck (Minor)**  Draw a card to gain advantage on a Deception or Performance check; the deck vanishes after 3 uses.

**5 Tickets**

- **Festival Cloak**  Once per long rest, cast *disguise self*.

- **Echo Bell**  Ring it to gain advantage on Perception checks for 10 minutes (1/day).

- **Concord Mark (Temporary)**  A stamped sigil recognized by guards; grants advantage on one social check with authorities.

### 🟣 Rare Festival Prizes (6–8 Tickets)

Strong but controlled rewards; these feel *special*.

**6 Tickets**

- **Lantern of Guiding Light**  Once per day, you may reroll a failed saving throw.

- **Ashen Ward Charm**  Once per long rest, gain resistance to the next instance of fire or force damage.

- **Boots of the Swift Crowd**  Ignore difficult terrain caused by crowds or clutter.

**7 Tickets**

- **Hero’s Keepsake**  A relic tied symbolically to a Fallen Dawn hero. Once per session, gain advantage on a roll that reflects that hero’s theme (DM adjudicates).

- **Carnival Favor**  Call in a minor NPC favor later in the campaign (lodging, info, safe passage).

**8 Tickets**

- **Festival Relic (Player Choice)**  A minor magic item of your choosing (subject to DM approval, uncommon tier).

### 🔴 Legendary Festival Prize (9–10 Tickets)

For groups that go *all in* on the festival.

**10 Tickets**

- **The Dawnbound Token**  Once per campaign arc, turn a failed roll into a success *or* negate a critical hit against you. The token visibly cracks after use.

### 🎭 DM Flavor Tips

- Prizes are presented by **masked attendants** who never remove their masks

- The prize tent feels *slightly magical* — time feels slower inside

- Some prizes may later show subtle connections to heroes or villains (foreshadowing!)
