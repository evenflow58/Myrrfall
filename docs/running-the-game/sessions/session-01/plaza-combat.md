# Festival Plaza Combat

**Setup Narrative (Read Aloud):**  The festival square is alive with color and music. Lanterns sway overhead, stalls overflow with treats, and performers twist through the crowd.

Suddenly, your attention is drawn to a small group of robed figures slipping quietly between the festival-goers. They move with a strange coordination, their hands occasionally glowing with faint, geometric sigils. They glance at one another, then at the nearby city guards, and their eyes flare with quiet purpose.

The city guards—stalwart, armored, and disciplined—stand watch over the crowd, glancing briefly at the figures. They do **not intervene** unless civilians are threatened. The robed figures, however, clearly intend to test the limits of festival security.

“The rules of order bend… only slightly,” one of them mutters, voice almost lost in the music. “Let’s see who notices.”

The plaza has become a controlled battlefield: **civilians are panicked, stalls block paths, and magical lantern posts hum with energy**. It is up to the party to act.

### Battlefield Features

| Feature | Details / Mechanics |
| --- | --- |
| Lantern Posts (4) | AC 12, HP 10, faint magical pulses. Hit by Concord energy: explode in 5-ft burst of arcane energy (Dex save DC 12, 1d6 force). Can be disabled with DC 12 Arcana check. |
| Food Stalls | Half cover; can be overturned to create 10-ft line of difficult terrain. |
| Panicked Crowd | Difficult terrain until cleared. A character can use an action (Persuasion or Intimidation, DC 12) to calm one quadrant. |
| City Guards (4) | Form perimeter; do not attack. Cast Defensive Shield (reaction, initiative 20) to reduce damage from explosive effects or protect civilians. |

### Enemies: Low-Level Concord Operatives

**Concord Initiate (3–4)**

- AC 12 \| HP 11 \| Speed 30 ft

- **Actions:**

  - **Arcane Bolt:** +3 to hit, 60 ft, 1d6 force damage

  - **Binding Glyph (Recharge 5–6):** Choose 5-ft square; DC 12 Dex save or target is restrained until next turn.

- **Tactics:**

  - Spread out to control movement.

  - Target hazards instead of PCs first (e.g., lantern posts, stalls) to create chaos and test reactions.

  - Avoid clustering, threaten area more than PCs directly.

**Concord Apprentice Leader (1)**

- AC 13 \| HP 20 \| Speed 30 ft

- **Actions:**

  - **Command Pulse:** Recharge 5–6; 10-ft radius, DC 12 Dex save or restrained for 1 round + takes 1d6 force damage

  - Directs Initiates (adds +1 to attack rolls for one Initiate per turn)

- **Tactics:**

  - Avoids civilians directly; focuses on testing party response.

  - If PCs overreact and harm civilians, the Leader may **retreat strategically**.

### Combat Flow / Hooks

- **Round 1:** The Concord Initiates activate glyphs near lantern posts and food stalls, testing PC reaction. Civilians panic.

- **Player Options Beyond “Attack”:**

  1. **Arcana**: Suppress glyphs (DC 12)

  1. **Athletics**: Move or shove stalls

  1. **Persuasion / Intimidation**: Calm crowd or force hesitating Initiates to pause

  1. **Stealth / Deception**: Observe without provoking Initiates

- **Escalation Mechanic (Optional, Round 3):** Roll 1d4:

  1. Lantern hums dangerously (potential 1d6 force burst)

  1. Stall topples, blocking path

  1. Civilians cluster (more difficult terrain)

  1. City guards tighten perimeter (Initiates get disadvantage next round)

### City Guards Role

- Focus on **protecting civilians**, not attacking the Concord.

- Cast **Defensive Shield** automatically to reduce environmental hazard damage by 2 (min 0).

- Issue verbal warnings to PCs if they target Initiates directly near civilians.

### Narrative Flavor During Combat

- Concord Operatives: calm, precise, almost clinical in their movements.

- They do not attack festival-goers directly; their attacks are **tests** rather than chaos.

- PCs notice **subtle geometric sigils on their robes**, hinting at the Concord’s secret hand in city politics.

- Civilians panic realistically: some hide, some cheer for PCs, giving a sense of urgency.

**Example Read-Aloud:**

Sparks dance along a lantern post as an Initiate gestures. One of the city guards steps in, shield glowing faintly, holding back the energy just long enough for a child to dart past. The Initiate nods to their companions, eyes glinting as if assessing your response. Will you intervene, observe, or try something clever?

### Ending the Fight

- When all Concord Initiates are subdued, **they flee quietly**, leaving subtle traces:

  - A sigil burned into a lantern

  - A cryptic note in a pocket: “The test was only beginning.”

- PCs can **choose to report the incident to city guards** or keep it to themselves.

- **Clock Impact:**

  - Resurgence: +1 minor tick (hidden Concord pressure)

  - Revelation: +1 minor tick if PCs investigate clues

- Tie to later sessions: these operatives were **testing both the PCs and festival security**, hinting at the **Concord’s manipulations behind the scenes**.

## CLOCK INTERACTION

- Minor villain incidents tick **Resurgence** and **Revelation** slightly

- Player interactions with festival challenges can advance **Revelation** (learning about heroes)

- The festival itself is safe; only optional conflicts advance the world’s danger
