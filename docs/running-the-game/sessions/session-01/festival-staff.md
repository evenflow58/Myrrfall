# Festival Staff

### Ticket Taker

**Name:** Marn Vickle **Role:** Ticket Counter & Prize Redeemer **Description:**  A harried human with ink-stained fingers, surrounded by dangling prizes and a suspiciously heavy lockbox.

**Personality:** Overworked, fair, surprisingly sharp **Flavor Line:**

“Tickets don’t lie. People do. What’ll it be—trinket or glory?”

**Use:**

- Explains ticket rewards

- Notices cheating or unusual behavior

- Can vouch for the PCs later (“They earned those”)

### Bystander 1

**Name:** Lora Fenwick **Description:**  A baker on her day off, juggling pastries and her young child.

**Flavor Line:**

“Heroes or not, it’s nice to believe someone’s watching out for us, isn’t it?”

**Use:**

- Represents ordinary citizens

- Will panic during violence

- Guards react strongly if she’s threatened

### Bystander 2

**Name:** Tamsin Quell **Description:**  A bored-looking teenager leaning against a post, pretending not to care.

**Flavor Line:**

“Bet the Fallen Dawn were just exaggerations. Still… kinda wish they were real.”

**Use:**

- Cynical contrast to hero worship

- May idolize PCs after the fight

- Easy hook for rumors spreading post-session
