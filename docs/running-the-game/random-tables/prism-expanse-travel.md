# Prism Expanse Travel Encounters

**Context:** The party is traveling toward the eastern pylon. The Expanse is unstable, magic pulses through the crystal terrain, and small creatures or environmental hazards react unpredictably. Roll a d8 for a random encounter. Each includes **salvage opportunities** and **DCs**.

| d8 | Event | Description / Player Options | Skill / DC | Salvage Found (Shattermarket-Aligned) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cracked Prism Shards | Jagged shards stick out; stepping wrong could hurt. Some are loose and can be removed. | Acrobatics DC 12 / Athletics DC 11 to navigate; Investigation DC 12 to spot weak shards | Prismglass Fragment ×1–2 |
| 2 | Prismatic Fog | A patch of fog refracts light, disorienting vision. | Perception DC 12 / Insight DC 12 to navigate; Arcana DC 13 to stabilize | Vial of Wild Residue ×1 |
| 3 | Wild Surge Wisps | Tiny motes of energy drift unpredictably, hovering around party members. | Dexterity DC 13 to avoid, Arcana DC 12 to disperse | Intact Prism Shard ×1 |
| 4 | Glasswood Tendril | Erratic sapwood branch curls toward the party. | Athletics DC 12 to dodge, Arcana DC 13 or Nature DC 12 to stabilize | Pylon Component ×1 |
| 5 | Skittering Shardlings | Small crystal creatures snatch at gear or block paths. | Persuasion / Intimidation DC 12 to chase away, Dexterity DC 12 to avoid | Fractured Lens Fragment ×1 |
| 6 | Energy Vortex Patch | A small swirl of unstable magic pulses from the ground. | Arcana DC 13 to dampen, Acrobatics DC 12 to jump past | Wild Residue Cluster ×1 |
| 7 | Resonant Cracks | Crystal terrain hums with harmonic pulses; stepping wrong amplifies resonance. | Insight DC 12 / Arcana DC 13 to predict safe spots, Athletics DC 12 / Acrobatics DC 12 to cross | Damaged Pylon Component ×1 |
| 8 | Fleeing Prism Beetles | Tiny crystalline insects scuttle from a pulse, blocking paths. | Nature DC 12 / Arcana DC 12 to calm, Dexterity DC 12 to dodge | Prism Jerky ×1 |

## Rolling the Travel Encounter with Instability

1. Roll **d8** for the encounter.

1. Resolve the encounter as usual (skill checks, DCs).

1. Update IP based on success/failure.

1. Optional: roll a **cumulative effect table** based on current IP level.

### Cumulative Effect Table (Optional)

Roll **1d4** if IP ≥ 4 after each encounter:

| d4 | Effect | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mini Surge | A burst of energy pulses from the ground; all characters make Dex DC 12 or take 1d4 lightning/force. |
| 2 | Aggitated Creatures | Any small creatures in the area (beetles, shardlings) swarm aggressively. Dexterity / Arcana DC 12 to avoid interaction. |
| 3 | Shifting Terrain | Crystal shards or glasswood shifts underfoot. Acrobatics / Athletics DC 12 to avoid falling or damaging gear. |
| 4 | Resonance Pulse | Arcana / Insight DC 12 to identify; failure → temporary disadvantage (-1 to next skill roll) or minor gear damage (one item of choice loses effectiveness until repaired). |

Repeat as party moves; higher IP → GM can **increase damage, frequency, or DCs slightly**.
