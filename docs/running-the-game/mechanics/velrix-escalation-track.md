# Velrix Escalation Track

The longer the party waits to confront him, the more he adapts.

Track this quietly.

### If Ignored After 1 Clue

He:

- Removes records from Shattermarket.

- Begins stockpiling unstable shards.

- Plants rumors that pylons suppress “storm evolution.”

### After 2 Clues

He:

- Makes contact with Ashen Remnant field agents.

- Begins experimenting outside town perimeter.

- Gains access to a portable harmonic amplifier.

Mechanically: He gains access to surge-based abilities later.

### After 3 Clues

He:

- Plants a catalyst in another pylon.

- Causes a surge during a repair attempt.

- Publicly blames Kular’s team.

Now stakes are personal.

### If Never Confronted Before Finale

Velrix becomes:

Storm-Touched Velrix Partially infused with prismatic surge energy.

He believes:

“The dragon isn’t coming. We’re building it.”
