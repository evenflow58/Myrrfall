# Character Questions

## Player questions

## 1. Why would your character want to go on an adventure?

## 2. Motivation & Momentum

*(Why they adventure — willingly or not)*

You already have the most important one. Here are a few that deepen it:

- **What problem would your character rather ignore—but can’t?**

- **What would make your character stop adventuring immediately?**

- **Who benefits if your character succeeds—and who benefits if they fail?**

- **Did your character choose this life, or did it choose them?**

## 3. Beliefs, Values & Fault Lines

*(What they believe—especially when tested)*

These are gold in *World of Echos*, where ideals matter.

- **What is something your character believes is “necessary,” even if it’s cruel?**

- **What line would your character refuse to cross, no matter the outcome?**

- **What does your character think the world** ***gets wrong*****?**

- **If order and truth came into conflict, which would your character protect?**

## 4. Personal History (Without Lore Overload)

*(Backstory hooks you can reuse)*

Avoid “tell me your whole life.” Ask for **moments** instead.

- **Name one event your character never talks about. Why?**

- **Who once believed in your character when no one else did?**

- **Who was your character wrong about?**

- **What did your character leave behind when they started adventuring?**

## 5. Relationship to Power & Authority

*(How they’ll react to Concord guards, Aurelion, etc.)*

This campaign has *structured power*—knowing how PCs react is crucial.

- **How does your character feel about laws they didn’t help create?**

- **Has authority ever protected them—or failed them?**

- **Would your character accept safety at the cost of freedom?**

## 6. Secrets & Leverage

*(Not all secrets are dark—just useful)*

You don’t need betrayals. You need *pressure points*.

- **What is something true about your character that would change how others see them?**

- **What lie is your character currently living?**

- **What does your character fear being known for?**

You can use these subtly—no forced reveals.

## 7. Ties to the World (Without Spoilers)

*(Grounding them in the setting)*

Keep this light and flexible.

- **What place in the world feels like “home,” even if they can’t return?**

- **What legend or story did your character grow up hearing—and do they believe it?**

- **Has your character ever encountered something that felt “bigger than them”?**

## 8. The “GM Permission” Question (Very Important)

Ask this explicitly:

**Is there anything you want to explore with this character—or anything you** ***don’t*** **want touched?**

This:

- Builds trust

- Gives you creative freedom safely

- Prevents accidental discomfort
