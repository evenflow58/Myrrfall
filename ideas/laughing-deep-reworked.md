# The Laughing Deep (Reworked)

> TODO: This version conflicts with the canonical Laughing Deep. Decide whether it supersedes the canon page, should be merged selectively, or should remain an unused alternative.

An offshore sinkhole-prison carved into black limestone and chained to the seabed. From above it looks like a ringed fortress; below, it’s a vertical maze of cells, drowned tunnels, and echoing shafts where sound travels wrong. Laughter is common—rarely human.

## Core Premise

[The Laughing Deep](../docs/lore/locations/laughing-deep.md) doesn’t “investigate truth.” It **breaks people**. Prisoners are trapped in a slow erosion of self: memory blurs, fear turns to giggles, guilt becomes obsession. The jailers don’t need confessions—only compliance.

---

## Maelis Quill, Warden-on-Paper

[Maelis Quill](../docs/lore/heroes/maelis-quill.md) is competent and charismatic, but his authority is *borrowed*. **Why his hold is tenuous:**
- **The prison runs on a resource**: sanity-drain. Whoever controls the source controls the prison.

The guards are split into:
- **Quill loyalists** (order, containment)
- **The Undertone** (a clique of officers who “dose” prisoners for profit and favors)
- **Hired blades** paid by outside interests (including the [Gilded Knives](../docs/lore/factions/gilded-knives.md))

- Quill’s worst secret: he’s convinced himself the prison is a necessary evil, but he’s losing the ability to tell what’s real in his own halls. **Quill’s public line:** “The Laughing Deep is stable.” **Truth:** It’s one bad night from becoming an asylum with keys.

---

## The Sanity-Sapping “Thing”: The Chorus Below

The Deep is built around a sealed shaft called **the Throat**, plugged with sigils and iron ribs. Beneath it lives a colony-intelligence prisoners call **the Chorus Below**.

### What It Is

**The Mirthleeches**: pallid, lamprey-thin aberrations that feed on emotional spikes (panic, shame, longing). They don’t kill quickly—they *curate* breakdowns.

### How It Feeds

- The Chorus cannot reach everyone directly; it requires **conductors**:
- **Salt-iron bars** etched with runes that “carry” thoughts like wire.
- **Brine lanterns** (guard-issued) that hum at night, turning dreams into harvest.
- **The Undertone’s “calm draught”**—actually a sedative that lowers mental defenses.

### What Prisoners Experience

- Hearing your own voice laugh from another cell.
- Remembering crimes you never committed—then feeling guilty anyway.
- Waking up with wet footprints around your bunk.
- **The Joke**: a phrase that gets stuck in your head; once you repeat it aloud, the Chorus “finds” you more easily.

---

## Why the Jailers Use It

The prison’s real control system is **sanity management**:
- **Mild drain** makes prisoners docile, suggestible, eager to please.
- **Heavy drain** makes them incoherent—useful for burying inconvenient detainees.
- **Selective drain** is the terrifying part: prisoners who behave get “quiet nights,” and the disobedient get the laughing fits. Quill tolerates the system because it prevents riots—until recently, when it started feeding *too well*.

## Why the Gilded Knives Want the Laughing Deep

They’re not trying to “free prisoners.” They want the **mechanism**.

### Their angle (choose one)
1. **Memory laundering**: They can make captured agents “forget” names, routes, faces—then recover the information later via a handler. The prison becomes a *vault for secrets*.
2. **Manufacturing pliable assets**: Break a person, rebuild them with a new loyalty—classic sleeper creation, made faster with Chorus exposure.
3. **Blackmail economy**: If they can induce false guilt/memories, they can ruin reputations without evidence (“He confessed—many times.”).
4. **A weaponizable epidemic**: Bottle the Chorus resonance into a portable “laughing plague” to collapse rival gangs or city watch barracks.
5. **Hidden prisoner**: Someone important is in the Deep (or needs to be put there), and the Knives need control to access/erase them.

### How They’re Making Inroads

- They’ve bought or compromised **Undertone officers**.
- They smuggle in **gilded tuning forks**—arcane resonators that can *steer* where the Chorus feeds.
- They’re mapping the prison’s acoustic geometry (vents, shafts, flooded corridors) to create **safe routes** where the whispers don’t reach.

**Their endgame:** replace Quill with a “warden” who answers to them, and turn the prison into a **mind-mill**.

## Key Locations (usable at the table)
- **The Ringward** (upper fortress): administrative offices, visiting chamber, armory. Looks orderly; smells of brine and ink.
- **The Spiral Cells**: stacked tiers around the Throat. The lower you go, the funnier the jokes become—and the less funny they feel.
- **Lantern Gallery**: racks of humming brine lanterns. Guards swear it’s just “to keep the damp away.”
- **The Undertone Clinic**: calm-draught distillery, restraint chairs, confession desks (not for truth—just for *patterning*).
- **Floodways**: half-submerged maintenance tunnels. Sounds travel through water into bone.
- **The Throat**: sealed shaft with iron ribs and sigils. Sometimes the ribs *ring* like a bell.

## What’s Going Wrong

Pick 2–3 “active failures” to define the current arc:
- **Overfeeding**: prisoners are breaking faster; guards are getting infected with laughing fits.
- **Quill’s wards are failing**: not from age—someone is *re-tuning* them.
- **A new “saint” appeared**: a prisoner who laughs softly and makes others calm… then hollow.

---

## Adventure Hooks
1. **Extraction**: The party must pull someone out—alive and *still themselves*—before the Chorus finishes “editing” them.
2. **Containment breach**: A riot isn’t the danger; a mass silence is. Everyone stops talking. Then the prison starts laughing.
3. **Quill’s bargain**: Quill hires the party off-book to identify the Undertone/Knives infiltration without alerting his own staff.
4. **The Knives’ offer**: They promise a clean way to “turn off” the Chorus—if the party helps remove Quill.
5. **The missing ledger**: Quill’s records (names, transfers, “dose schedules”) are gone. Someone is rewriting who was ever imprisoned.

## Practical “Madness” Play

Instead of random madness tables, use a **3-step track** triggered by: nights in the Spiral Cells, exposure to brine lantern hum, hearing The Joke, or confronting the Throat.
- **Fray 1: Unsteady** – disadvantage on the next Insight check; recurring intrusive laughter in silence.
- **Fray 2: Unraveling** – short-term compulsion (repeat a phrase, tap the wall, avoid eye contact); one “false memory” the DM can invoke once.
- **Fray 3: Hollowed** – the Chorus can speak through you briefly; you lose 1 meaningful personal detail until restored (name of a friend, hometown, vow). Recovery is possible via: silence wards, true-name anchors, restoration magic, or meaningful roleplayed grounding (a remembered bond, a physical token, a confession that’s actually true).
