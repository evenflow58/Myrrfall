# Contributing

Keep canonical material under `docs/` and drafts, alternatives, and superseded concepts under `ideas/`.

Use lowercase kebab-case filenames, YAML front matter, focused pages, relative links, and a `Related Pages` section. Do not invent lore to fill gaps. When sources conflict and neither clearly supersedes the other, preserve both and add a TODO describing the conflict.

Before committing, verify relative links, navigation reachability, canonical status, and duplication.
